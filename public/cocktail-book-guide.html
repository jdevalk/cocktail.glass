Two sensible routes

Recommended purchase strategy

Fewest sensible orders

  1. One bol.com basket: 10 books. Buy the ten fairly priced physical editions; omit the overpriced used Craft of the Cocktail.
  2. One EU bookseller basket: 3–5 facsimiles. Use a German/EU fulfiller on AbeBooks and confirm that the same dealer supplies Jerry Thomas, Johnson, Waldorf and Café Royal reprints.
  3. One broad used-book order: 3 books. Ask one AbeBooks dealer to combine Esquire, Trader Vic and Embury in good vintage condition.
  4. Source four rare works individually: Savoy, Gentleman's Companion, So Red the Nose and a true vintage Jerry Thomas/Johnson only when the right copies appear.

Practical result: 3 core orders cover about 18 books; the final four are patient collector hunts. This avoids paying customs clearance on ordinary modern books.

Collector-focused strategy

  1. Buy all post-1998 books new from bol in one Dutch order.
  2. Choose 1948–58 Embury, 1947/48 Trader Vic, and 1949 Esquire as attainable vintage originals.
  3. For Savoy, favour a handsome 1950s–70s printing or the attractive 1987 facsimile unless €1,000+ is comfortable.
  4. Hunt original 1935 So Red the Nose and a 1946 Baker combined edition; accept jacketless copies to control cost.
  5. Use accurate facsimiles for Jerry Thomas, Johnson, Waldorf and Café Royal while waiting for genuine early copies.

Ask every non-EU dealer to declare the sale accurately. Dutch import VAT and carrier handling may apply; books usually benefit from the Netherlands' reduced VAT treatment, but the carrier's customs assessment controls.

Currency note. USD prices in cards use an indicative €0.86 per US$ and GBP prices €1.16 per £ for comparison only. Listings preserve their seller currency; shipping to the Netherlands must be confirmed where marked “quote”.
